## Fresher sitsfest 8.9. at 5pm @ Boombox

If you don't yet know where your fellow students are from, now is the perfect time to find out! For the Freshman Sitsfest, you get to dress in the style of your hometown. The theme is “Where Are You From?”, so dress freely and creatively, but try to represent your hometown or place of origin in your outfit.

Sitsit is an academic dinner party where we sing, eat and give not-so-rehearsed speeches. You don't need to know the rules of sitsit before attending the Freshman Sitsfest, as they will be explained and learned during the event. However, one thing to remember before you arrive is that being late is frowned upon!

The Freshman Sitsfest will be held at Boombox on Seminaarinkatu on Monday, September 8th, starting at 5 PM. Make sure to arrive on time so you have enough time to find your assigned seat!

What: Freshman Sitsfest
Where: Boombox – Musica
When: Tuesday, September 8th, 5:00 PM–10:00 PM
Why: To get familiar with sitsfest culture :)
Other: Bring your own bottle(s)
