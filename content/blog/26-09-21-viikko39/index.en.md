## Syysmetro 23.9. at 4pm -> @ Jyväskylä and nearing municipalities

Welcome to Syysmetro - ready to hop onboard?


🍂 Date: 23.9.2026
📍 Place: Jyväskylä and nearing municipalities


Syysmetro is a unique, appro-based, and interdisciplinary long-distance bar crawl.
Our dashing and daring tour guides will lead us to the hidden gems of Central Finland, which
are also known as village bars. However, we won’t be setting out on this journey alone, as our
expedition will be joined by our friends Linkki, Ynnä, Syrinx, Algo, Radikaali, Lingviestit, and Magna
Carta. Together, we’ll embark on a memorable fall adventure! 🍂

## European Researchers' Night 25.9. @ Jyväskylä

What is a researchers’ job like? What is happening in science right now? And how are sciences and research affecting our everyday life?

Come and find out at Researchers’ Night!

Researchers’ Night is an annual event, where researchers all over the Europe open their doors to every curious people. 

The all-day event celebrates achievements of science and research with multidisciplinary programme. Researchers present their job and 

studies via workshops, lectures, demonstrations and laboratory visits. There will be something interesting for everyone, regardless of age.

More info about the event can be found at https://tutkijoidenyo.fi/en/
