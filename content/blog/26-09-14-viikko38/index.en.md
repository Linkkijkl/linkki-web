## Fresher party 17.9. at 5pm -> @ Jyväskylä center

Yehaaw!

The moment you’ve all been waiting for is finally here: the Freshers’ Party on September 17th! Now it’s time for you to compete in various challenges and strive for fame and glory!

This year’s theme is Wild West, so dress accordingly—weather-appropriate and in the spirit of cowboys!

Come to Agoras frontyard at 16:50. You might also want to bring some something for the arrangers to enjoy in hopes of scoring extra points that way. Teams have until 21:00 to go around the checkpoints. After reaching the goal you can join the afterparty in 3MIOT. You can purchase ticket to the 3MIOT afterparty here (no 3MIOT overall patch included). You can only experience fresher party once, so you don’t want to miss this event!

## Syysmetro (Autumn metro) general ticket sale

🍂 Date: 23.9.2026
📍 Place: Jyväskylä and nearing municipalities

Our dashing and daring tour guides will lead us to the hidden gems of Central Finland, which

expedition will be joined by our friends Linkki, Ynnä, Syrinx, Algo, Radikaali, Lingviestit, and Magna
