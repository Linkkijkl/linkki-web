## Kauppakadun Appro 1.10. at 4:30pm -> Jyväskylä

Kauppakadun Appro is one of the most tradtitional student events in Finland held in Jyväskylä. The event is celebrating its 50th anniversary this year too!

Kauppakadun Appro is the best way to kick off the schoolyear with your studymates!

START

The starting location for the Appro is the Lutakko harbor square, from where participants set off to complete their challenge at restaurants and bars throughout Jyväskylä.

A variety of entertainment is planned at the starting point to get everyone in the right mood for the Appro.

THE APPRO

The concept behind Kauppakadun Appro is based on the idea of playfully earning academic degrees by collecting completion stamps in the Appro Pass.

This is done by collecting stamps in the Appro Pass at pre-selected restaurants, bars, and other locations in downtown Jyväskylä.
THE AFTERPARTY

The event culminates in the official afterparty at Paviljonki, where examiners evaluate the candidates’ performance.

At the afterparty, overalls patches corresponding to the degree earned are distributed in exchange for an Appropass. Headliners Minttu, Gettomasa, and Mikael Gabriel guarantee the best student experience in Finland.

**Where**: Start together 16:30
