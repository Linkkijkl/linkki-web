## Fresher bar tour 3.9. at 6pm -> @ Jyväskylä city center
Haven't found your favorite bar in Jyväskylä yet? Now would be the perfect time to see what kind of bar this town has to offer! On thursday 3rd of september we'll go touring Jyväskyläs bars!

The bar tour will be held in groups and each group will have their own route and timetable. The tour will begin in Kirkkopuisto in front of the church at 6pm, but please arrive at about 5:45pm so that we'll have enough time to be separated into groups.

If you're late, you can join the tour in any of the groups. Just ask in Linkki's telegam group where the groups are going and join along. The tour can also be done sober!

The tour is advertised as a fresher event, but older students are free to join as well!

**TL;DR:**
**What**: Linkki's fresher bar tour
**Where**: Gatherin at Kirkkopuisto
**When**: Thursday 3rd of september at 6pm
**Why**: To get to know fellow students and Jyväskyläs bars
